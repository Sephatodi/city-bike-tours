# How to book a City Bike Tour — Easy Guide

Welcome! This guide will walk you through signing up and booking a fun city bike tour. Just follow each step. You've got this!

---

## Step 1 — Open the website

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, etc.).
2. Type the website address in the bar at the top and press **Enter**.
3. You should see the home page with pictures of bikes and tours.

---

## Step 2 — Make an account (sign up)

You need an account before you can book a ride.

1. Look for a button that says **Sign in** or **Sign up** (usually in the top-right corner) and click it.
2. You will see two tabs: **Create account** and **Sign in**. Click **Create account**.
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Full name** — your first and last name.
   - **Email** — your email address.
   - **Phone** — your phone number (this one is optional, you can skip it).
   - **Password** — pick a secret password with at least 8 characters. Don't tell anyone your password!
4. Click the **Create my account** button.

---

## Step 3 — Sign in

Now sign in with the account you just made.

1. Click **Sign in** (or the same button you used before).
2. Make sure the **Sign in** tab is selected.
3. Type your **email** and your **password**.
4. Click **Sign in**.

---

## Step 4 — Pick a ride

1. On the home page, click **Book a ride**, **View schedule**, or **Book now**.
2. You will see a list of bike routes with names, pictures, prices, and start times.
3. Click on the ride you want.

---

## Step 5 — Choose your date and time

1. Pick a **date** from the calendar.
2. Pick a **start time** from the list.
3. How many people are going? Use the **riders** button to pick a number (1, 2, 3, and so on).

---

## Step 6 — Add your details and pay

1. Fill in your **name**, **phone number**, and **email**.
2. If there is a section for notes (like "I need a small bike"), you can type something there — or leave it blank.
3. If you are paying online, enter your card details. Otherwise, you can choose to pay on the day.
4. Click the **Book** or **Confirm booking** button.

---

## Step 7 — You're all set!

After you book:

- You will get a **confirmation screen** with a booking reference number. Write it down or take a screenshot.
- You may get an **email** or **text message** with your ride pass and a QR code.
- Save the QR code on your phone — you will need it on the day of your ride.

---

## On the day of your ride

1. Arrive **15 minutes early** at the meeting point.
2. Show your **QR code** to the guide so they can check you in.
3. Put on a helmet (the guide will provide one).
4. Have fun! 🚲

---

## If you need help

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| I forgot my password | Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the steps. |
| I can't sign in | Make sure your email and password are typed correctly. Caps Lock might be on! |
| I want to cancel | Open your account, find the booking, and look for a **Cancel** button. Cancellations are free if you do it more than 24 hours before the ride. |
| Something is wrong | Ask an adult, or contact the tour company using the phone number or email on the website. |

---

## Tips for a great ride

- Wear comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes.
- Bring a water bottle and sunscreen.
- Bring a phone with a charged battery so you can show your QR code.
- A grown-up should be with you if you are using public transport to get to the meeting point.

Have an awesome ride! 🚴‍♂️
